Frequently Asked Questions & Guides

How-to guides for UptimeBunny website, API, SSL & Linux server monitoring
❓ What is UptimeBunny and how does it work? ▼
UptimeBunny is a 24/7 uptime monitoring platform for websites, web applications, APIs, SSL certificates, and Linux servers. It performs continuous automated HTTP/HTTPS pings and TCP port checks. When your website or server experiences downtime or high latency, UptimeBunny immediately alerts you via Push Notifications, Email, and Telegram.
🖥️ How do I install the Linux Server Agent (Nginx, PM2, Gunicorn, PostgreSQL)? ▼
Open the UptimeBunny app or web dashboard, navigate to Servers, copy your single-command installer script, and run it on your Linux terminal (Ubuntu / Debian / CentOS / RHEL):

curl -sSL https://uptimebunny.com/install-agent.sh | bash -s -- --key YOUR_API_KEY --services nginx,apache2,pm2,gunicorn,postgres,mysql,redis

The agent automatically detects active services, collects CPU %, RAM, Disk, Load averages, and streams service log histories to your dashboard every 60 seconds.

🔒 How does SSL Certificate Monitoring work? ▼
UptimeBunny automatically inspects the TLS/SSL certificate of every HTTPS monitor. It checks valid issuer credentials, expiration dates, and cipher protocols, sending warning alerts at 30 days, 15 days, and 1 day before expiration.
🔔 How do I configure Instant Telegram & Email Outage Alerts? ▼
Go to Alerting / Settings in your dashboard or app. Enter your email address or Telegram Chat ID. You will receive real-time notifications with downtime duration, diagnostic status codes, and recovery notifications as soon as your service comes back UP.
🗑️ How do I delete my account or monitor history? ▼
You can purge your account and monitor history at any time from your account settings in the app or by visiting our Account Deletion Page.
