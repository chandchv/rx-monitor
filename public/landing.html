Application Overview

What is UptimeBunny?

UptimeBunny is a continuous 24/7 website and server uptime monitoring application. It performs periodic HTTP/HTTPS pings and TCP port checks against your configured target URLs to ensure your digital infrastructure stays online. If your website or API suffers an outage or severe latency, UptimeBunny instantly relays push notifications, email alerts, and Telegram messages so you can fix issues immediately.

📡 1. Continuous Uptime Pings

Performs automated HTTP status checks, monitors SLA response times, and alerts you before your customers notice downtime.

🔔 2. Multi-Channel Alerts

Delivers real-time notifications via Mobile Push, Email, and Telegram when your website or SSL certificate encounters errors.

🔑 3. Google Sign-In Usage

We use Google Sign-In solely to securely verify your email address and authenticate your account to route your downtime alerts.
