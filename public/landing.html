Application Overview

What is UptimeBunny?

UptimeBunny is a continuous 24/7 website and server uptime monitoring application. It performs periodic HTTP/HTTPS pings and TCP port checks against your configured target URLs to ensure your digital infrastructure stays online. If your website or API suffers an outage or severe latency, UptimeBunny instantly relays push notifications, email alerts, and Telegram messages so you can fix issues immediately.

Help & Guides

Frequently Asked Questions & How-To's

Everything you need to know about setting up website monitoring, Linux server agents, and notifications.

❓ What is UptimeBunny and how does it work? ▼

UptimeBunny is a 24/7 uptime monitoring service for websites, APIs, SSL certificates, and Linux servers. It performs automated HTTP/HTTPS pings and TCP check requests every 10 to 60 seconds. When your target website or server suffers an outage or response time spike, UptimeBunny immediately sends push notifications, email alerts, and Telegram messages so you can resolve issues before your customers notice.

🖥️ How do I install the Linux Server Agent (Nginx, PM2, Gunicorn, PostgreSQL)? ▼

Go to the Servers menu in the mobile app or dashboard, copy your one-line installer command, and run it on your Linux terminal (Ubuntu / Debian / CentOS):
curl -sSL https://uptimebunny.com/install-agent.sh | bash -s -- --key YOUR_API_KEY --services nginx,apache2,pm2,gunicorn,postgres,mysql,redis
The agent automatically detects active services, collects CPU, RAM, Disk, Load averages, and streams service log histories to your dashboard every 60 seconds.

🎁 How do I redeem a Beta Promo Code for 1 Year of Free Premium? ▼

Open the UptimeBunny app or web dashboard, go to Settings > Manage Subscription, scroll to Redeem Beta Access Code, type your invitation or promotional code, and tap Redeem. Your account will instantly upgrade to Premium.

🔒 How does SSL Certificate Monitoring work? ▼

UptimeBunny automatically checks the TLS/SSL certificate of every HTTPS monitor. It tracks valid issuer details, encryption protocols, and expiration dates, automatically sending warning alerts at 30 days, 15 days, and 1 day before expiration so you never let an expired SSL certificate take your site down.

🔔 How do I configure Instant Telegram & Email Outage Alerts? ▼

In your UptimeBunny dashboard or app settings, navigate to Alerting. Enter your recipient email address or Telegram Chat ID. You will receive real-time notifications with downtime duration, diagnostic status codes, and recovery notifications as soon as your service comes back UP.

🗑️ How do I request account deletion or data removal? ▼

You can delete your account and all associated monitor histories at any time from your account settings in the mobile app or by visiting our public Account Deletion Page.
